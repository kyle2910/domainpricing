Frequently asked questions

What is WHOIS?

WHOIS is a public protocol used to look up the registered owner, registrar, registration date, and expiration date of a domain name. This tool uses RDAP (the modern replacement for WHOIS) to fetch that data directly from the domain registry.

Why can't I see the owner's personal contact info?

Since GDPR and most registrars' privacy policies took effect, personal registrant details (name, email, address) are redacted by default in public WHOIS/RDAP records. Only registrar, dates, status, and nameservers remain publicly visible for most domains.
